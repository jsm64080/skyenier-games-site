OUR GAMES

Pseudoworker is a 2D-3D hybrid game where you, playing as the dumb, but ambitious Henry, fill in for your twin brother Roscoe for a shift at the infamous fine-dining, sports bar, cafe infused restaurant L'Anomalie…

... with zero restaurant experience.

On top of that? Despite being identical twins, Henry and Roscoe couldn't be more different! Roscoe is a model worker and has been working there for over 4 years! Henry is so focused on "having fun" that he has never held a job for more than a week! This isn't any normal day to make such a stupid decision either. The restaurant is totally packed, and Roscoe's crush, Elaine, who works the host desk, has her heart set on confessing her feelings- TODAY!

And don't expect any directions, you've been working there for 4 years, right?

You are the cog running in this restaurant.
Do you do your best to wing it and blend in?
Be yourself and cause chaos?
Find a way for Elaine to confess her feelings to the right person?
Just sit and watch football in the bathroom?
Help someone find direction and meaning in their life?
Team up on a heist to free a bunch of crabs into the ocean before they get boiled?

Really, I'm serious. It's up to you.

(Game is still in early development, release date TBD.)
